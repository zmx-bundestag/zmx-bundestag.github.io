Parties Inroduction

EmoZ

As one of the oldest and largest parties in our Senate, EmoZ is arguably the most influencial and the longest lasting among all other parties. Upon it's creation way back in time in 2024, it's ideology was to make our school an open, heterogeous, and diverse place. Now as the party develops, members start to grow a bit apart, with people of both right and left wing, mxing together a moderate right political party. This party is in favor of having a democratic government, and is quite transversal. EmoZ currently has the largest amount of seats in office, and its members have been elected as both chancellor and president, making it the most powerful party as well. Yet interestingly, this party was disintergrated by its leader Jonathan for mysterious reasons, making it the most short-lived party so far.

SDPE

Being the youngest party in the senate, the Socialist Democratic Party of EmoZ is one of the many pieces that broke out after the disintergration of EmoZ party. It has a clear socialist orientation and aims to assimilate everyone into the socialist family, and is a major ally of the MMGA Party. It's leader, Max, is a german who had special advantages in his speech and writing skills, and is able to appear at most crucial events to give inspiring speeches, making him very well-known in the senate. On the other hand, this party is more aligned towards Stalinism, with a lot of authoritarianity, to gain more control over the government when they win the elections.

JL

JL started as a corporation that made a wide variety of products, ranging from sport equipments to military research. However, with the coming of this Senate, and the leaving of their previous leader, the party disassembled, and lost most of its members. Another quite conservative party, and also in favor of capitalism due it's successful history as a big corporation.

MMGA

MMGA (Make ZMX Math Great Again) is a new political party formed by the merge of Gwo and ZoyuE. Advocating for equality, diversity, and prosperity, it seeks to create a peaceful, engaging math education system where all students thrive. While rooted in left-wing liberalism, MMGA blends socialist ideals with conservative pragmatism, positioning itself as a moderate yet progressive force—committed to reform without ideological extremism. Their leader, Henry, will soon be leaving the political realm of our senate, the struggle of power that is about to play out will be a true sight to see. Henry also have a special interest in launching coup d'etats against the previous government, but he instead suceeded through legal means in the end.

Dab

Dab is a new party created along with the formation of this government, and although it's just start, we can already have a peek at its political orientation. This party aims to make everyone happy, and makes a satire about nearly anything in existance. However, also due to it's very interesting and non-fitting politics, it is quite isolated with only one member. Having a strong ally like EmoZ can be very beneficial to this party.

议会历史

在初中的尘埃中，高中诞生了，我们也随着时间流逝又升了一级，经过一系列重组以及微调，9年级的班级格局大体形成，随着9/1日开学以及一些新鲜血液的灌入，我们议会的历史算是正式拉开了帷幕。

9/26日，经过了进1个月的磨合后，zmx建立起了以自己的绝对权威为核心的君主专制班级，并将michelle设置为了一个学生合作者以巩固支持，zmx会每天发放以及批改作业，以求学生的学生的学术增长，但也在无形中压迫着学生，挤压着学生的自由。

后记

很多时间的具体内容和时间已经无法考证了，比如bingbing哪一天第一次给我们增加作业，“去后面站着”一开始是针对谁的，和一个潜在的最后一任黑板工。事实证明我又低估了工作量，我们刷的话实在太多了。。难怪zmx会不要再刷瓶了，尤其是4月份到6月这段时间，每翻一天聊天记录都是顶级折磨。然而，当真得到了暑假没什么人说话的时候，我却又觉得惋惜——一个时代就这么悄无声息的消逝了。诚然，一大堆的聊天记录看起来很头疼，但是我看也没什么，起码挺热闹。（）这是一种不可名状的感受，有失落、有哀伤、有无奈。我们还在be gay的时候，两个月都显得如此漫长，现在回头再看，一年就这么过去了，令人无比感慨。本来寻思又整个长篇大论出来结果没啥好说的了，那就到这里吧。以后的事，以后再说吧。依旧，感谢所有人，感谢你们的包容，感谢你们的陪伴，以及感谢所有人提供的社会学研究样本（ 卡尔·马克思说过，人是所有社会关系的总和，只要还有人，就仍然存在一个社会，人只要还在社会中就无法避免被社会所影响，哪怕只有一个人。我们之间或许并没有千丝万缕的联系，但是我们遇见过，这就足够了。回到前言的问题，这一切真的有意义吗？我会说，或许，存在就是意义；或许，这一切根本就不需要意义。我不知道，但时间会回答我们。。。
